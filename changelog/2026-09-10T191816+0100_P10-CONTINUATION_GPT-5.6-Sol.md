### Volatile target lifecycle

- Added a capacity-bounded in-memory target registry; targets are not written to SQLite, memory, traces, or project logs.
- Target acquisition performs a fresh exact Level 0 observation and rejects missing, ambiguous, truncated-uncertain, offscreen, rectangle-less, or identity-less results.
- Targets carry owner/device/project scope, process-start identity, root and native window handles, UIA runtime/automation identity, physical rectangle, topology revision, and an expiry of at most five minutes.
- Revalidation consumes a distinct exact target-scoped Level 1 or Level 2 grant, reruns UIA, and rechecks grant/approval evidence after the child returns.
- Process restart, closed windows, disappeared elements, ambiguous identities, display changes, expiry, device mismatch, revocation, exhaustion, and in-flight authority changes fail closed.

### Walk, turn, and point presentation

- Added target-bound Level 1 presentation through the existing P09 command channel.
- `walk`, `turn`, and `point` map to `move_to`, `look_at`, and `show_marker` respectively.
- Commands use revalidated current geometry and topology; moved controls retarget and disappeared controls enqueue nothing.
- Embodiment command authority now retains the validated evidence object so delivery rechecks the exact operation/resource and permits only the already-consumed authorized use.
- Added Core routes and SDK methods for target acquisition, revalidation, and presentation.
- Added no focus, pointer, keyboard, UIA invocation, or other UI mutation.
