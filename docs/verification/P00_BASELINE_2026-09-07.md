## Conclusion and limits

P00 now has a reproducible SORA development baseline, a dated prototype reuse/gap map, a validated
private backup and restore path, and an explicit naming/migration decision. Runtime contracts,
legacy data import, real persistence, provider behavior, and the message-to-patch vertical slice are
not implemented; they remain P01–P04 work.
